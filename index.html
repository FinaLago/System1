Security & Privacy

Review your account protection and manage your data.

Password Protection

Passwords are protected with browser-side PBKDF2 hashing in this prototype. Production authentication and password storage must be handled by a secure backend.

Use a unique password with at least 8 characters. Never share your password.

Session Timeout

FinaLago automatically logs you out after 15 minutes of inactivity on this device.

15-minute inactivity timeout

Data Encryption

This website uses HTTPS when served through GitHub Pages to protect data in transit. Backup files can also be encrypted with a passphrase. Full database encryption at rest requires a secure backend and database.

Data Backup & Recovery

Create an encrypted backup of this browser's FinaLago data, or restore a backup file on this device.

Keep your backup passphrase safe. FinaLago cannot recover a forgotten backup passphrase.

Privacy Notice & Consent

FinaLago collects account details and the business information or documents you choose to submit so personnel can process requested financial services, communicate with you, and maintain service records. Access should be limited to authorized personnel. Do not upload unnecessary sensitive information.

In this prototype, information is stored in your browser. Production deployment must provide a complete privacy policy, retention and deletion procedures, a secure backend, and a contact point for privacy requests.
